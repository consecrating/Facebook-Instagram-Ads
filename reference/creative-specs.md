# Creative Specs & What Actually Works

> ⚠️ **Meta changes specs and character limits regularly.** Treat this as practical guidance, and trust the preview in Ads Manager over any document — including this one. If the preview looks wrong, it is wrong.

---

## Aspect ratios — the only three you need

| Ratio | Pixels | Where it shows | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **4:5** | 1080 × 1350 | Facebook + Instagram Feed | ⭐ Best feed default — occupies more screen than 1:1 |
| **9:16** | 1080 × 1920 | Reels, Stories | ⭐ Cheapest inventory, make this always |
| **1:1** | 1080 × 1080 | Feed, safe everywhere | Good fallback |

**Minimum viable setup: one 4:5 and one 9:16 per ad.** Ads Manager lets you upload a different asset per placement — use it. A cropped-from-landscape image in a 9:16 slot looks amateurish and performs like it.

**Never use landscape (16:9) as your primary creative.** Mobile feed is vertical; landscape wastes most of the screen.

### Safe zones for 9:16
Stories and Reels overlay UI on top of your creative. **Keep text and faces within the middle ~70%** — roughly 250px clear at top and 340px at bottom. Text placed in those bands gets covered by the profile name, CTA button and progress bar.

---

## Text limits — practical guidance

| Field | Practical limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary text** | First ~125 characters visible | Everything after is behind "…more". **Put your hook in the first line** |
| **Headline** | ~27–40 characters | Truncates on smaller screens — front-load the meaning |
| **Description** | ~30 characters, often hidden | Don't rely on it |

**The single most important formatting rule: your hook must survive truncation.** Assume nobody clicks "…more". Write as if the first line is all you get.

```
❌ "We are a leading service provider in the city with over 10 years of
    experience and our team of certified…"     ← hook is buried

✅ "AC not cooling even after service?"
    ← everything that matters is in line 1
```

## Images

- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Resolution:** 1080px on the shortest side minimum
- **File size:** keep under ~30 MB
- **Text on image:** Meta no longer hard-enforces the old 20% text rule, but **heavy text still tends to suppress reach and looks like spam.** Keep text minimal and large
- Use **real photos** of your work, premises, products, staff. In India, authenticity outperforms stock decisively — stock photography reads as untrustworthy
- One clear focal point. Busy collages fail at thumbnail size

**Test at thumbnail size.** Shrink your image to 25% and look at it on a phone. If you can't tell what it is, neither can your customer.

## Video — where the results are

Video is generally your cheapest route to attention on Meta, and phone-shot video frequently outperforms polished production because it doesn't read as an advertisement.

### Specs
- **Ratio:** 9:16 primary, 4:5 or 1:1 secondary
- **Length:** 15–30s for most; up to 60s if genuinely engaging
- **Format:** MP4 or MOV, H.264
- **Captions:** **always.** A large share of feed viewing is muted
- **Sound:** design so it works silently, and rewards unmuting

### The structure that works

```
0–3s     HOOK. A problem or claim that stops the scroll.
         This decides everything — most viewers leave here.
3–15s    Show the problem specifically. Make them recognise themselves.
15–40s   What you do. Show real work, real results.
40–55s   Who you are, where, price range.
55–60s   One clear CTA.
```

### Hook patterns that work in India

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Recognisable problem | "AC not cooling even after service?" |
| Price | "AC service from ₹499 in Jaipur" |
| Speed | "Repaired today. Same-day slots." |
| Proof | "1,200+ ACs serviced this year" |
| Objection | "No hidden charges. Price confirmed first." |
| Direct question | "Paying twice for the same repair?" |

**Say it in the first 3 seconds or don't say it.**

## Formats

| Format | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Single image** | Simple offers, local | Fastest to make |
| **Single video** | Almost everything | ⭐ Usually the best performer |
| **Carousel** | Multiple products/angles | Good for e-commerce and browsing intent |
| **Collection** | Catalog + storefront feel | Needs a catalog |
| **Flexible / Dynamic creative** | Letting Meta mix assets | Useful, but harder to read which asset won |

**At low budget, keep it simple:** one video ad, one static ad, one alternate-angle ad. Three ads, one ad set. That's the P00 structure and it's correct.

## Call-to-action buttons

Match the CTA to the actual next step. Mismatch costs you conversions.

| Goal | CTA |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp chat | **Send WhatsApp Message** / **Send Message** |
| Instant Form | **Get quote**, **Apply now**, **Sign up** |
| Website enquiry | **Learn more**, **Get quote** |
| E-commerce | **Shop now** |
| Booking | **Book now** |
| App | **Install now**, **Download** |

**"Learn more" is the safe default** when nothing else fits — it promises little and disappoints nobody.

## Creative volume and rotation

| Daily budget | Ads live | Refresh |
|---|---|---|
| ₹200–500 | 3 | Every 2–3 weeks |
| ₹500–1,000 | 3–4 | Every 1–2 weeks |
| ₹1,000–2,000 | 4–6 | Weekly |
| ₹2,000+ | 6+ | Twice weekly |

**Frequency is your trigger.** Above ~3.0 on a 7-day window means you're re-showing the same people — refresh creative or widen the audience.

**Rotate the weakest ad, keep the winner.** Never replace all creatives at once; you lose your baseline and can't attribute the change.

## India-specific creative notes

1. **Real photos beat stock, decisively.** Scepticism about online sellers is high
2. **Show the price.** Filters out low-intent enquiries and builds trust
3. **Regional language faces less competition** — often cheaper per result
4. **Landmark references beat street addresses** — people navigate by landmark
5. **Captions are essential**, especially for regional-language video
6. **"COD available" lifts e-commerce conversion** — say it if true
7. **Avoid aggressive urgency.** It reads as a scam and costs trust
8. **Festive creative works**, but expect higher CPMs in those windows
9. **WhatsApp icon/mention in creative** signals low-friction contact
10. **Faces work.** Real people, especially you or your staff, outperform product-only shots for service businesses

## Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] 4:5 **and** 9:16 versions uploaded
- [ ] Hook is in the **first line** of primary text
- [ ] Headline meaning survives truncation
- [ ] Captions on all video
- [ ] Text/faces inside 9:16 safe zones
- [ ] Recognisable at thumbnail size
- [ ] CTA matches the real next step
- [ ] Price or range visible (recommended)
- [ ] Real photos, not stock
- [ ] **Previewed in Ads Manager on every placement**
