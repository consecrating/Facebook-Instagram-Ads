# Ad Copy Templates (India, ₹)

Fill in the brackets. Every template assumes the hook lives in the **first line**, because everything after ~125 characters hides behind "…more".

---

## The universal frame

```
[HOOK — line 1, must survive truncation]

[SPECIFIC OFFER with ₹ price or range]
✓ [Benefit]
✓ [Benefit]
✓ [Trust signal]

📍 [Locality + landmark]
[ONE clear CTA]
```

**Four rules that matter more than the wording:**
1. **Hook in line 1.** Assume nobody clicks "…more"
2. **Show the price.** Filters out low-intent enquiries; builds trust in a sceptical market
3. **Name the locality**, not just the city. "Vaishali Nagar" beats "Jaipur"
4. **One action.** Not "call, WhatsApp, DM or visit"

---

## Hook library

Pick a pattern, not a phrase.

| Pattern | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Recognised problem | `[Problem] even after [attempted fix]?` | "AC not cooling even after service?" |
| Price-led | `[Service] from ₹[X] in [Locality]` | "AC service from ₹499 in Malviya Nagar" |
| Speed | `[Outcome] today. [Availability].` | "AC repaired today. Same-day slots." |
| Proof | `[Number]+ [customers] in [City] [timeframe]` | "1,200+ ACs serviced in Jaipur this year" |
| Objection | `No [common fear]. [Reassurance].` | "No hidden charges. Price confirmed first." |
| Direct question | `[Uncomfortable question]?` | "Paying twice for the same repair?" |
| Cost of inaction | `[Bad outcome] costs ₹[X]. [Prevention] costs ₹[Y].` | "A burnt compressor costs ₹18,000. A service costs ₹499." |

## Video script frame (30 seconds)

```
0-3s    "[HOOK — the problem, out loud]"
3-10s   "[Describe the problem specifically. Show it.]"
10-20s  "[What you do. Show real work.]"
20-27s  "We're [BUSINESS] in [LOCALITY]. [SERVICE] from ₹[PRICE]."
27-30s  "WhatsApp us — link below." [show number/CTA on screen]
```

**Captions always.** Speak the language your customers speak.

## WhatsApp reply templates

**First reply (automated greeting):**
```
Hi! Thanks for messaging [BUSINESS]. Checking this now.

Quick question so I can give you an exact price:
which area are you in?
```

**Qualifying:**
```
Got it. And roughly what budget range are you working with?
```

**Quote:**
```
For [their need] we'd do [specific approach], which includes
[X] and [Y]. That works out to ₹[PRICE].

Shall I book you in for [SPECIFIC SLOT]?
```

**Follow-up (within 24h — after that the free window closes):**
```
+2h:   Just checking you saw my message about [SERVICE]?
+8h:   Happy to answer anything — no obligation at all.
+20h:  I'll close this enquiry for now, but message anytime.
       Here's our price list for reference: [attach]
```

## Phrases to avoid in India

| ❌ Avoid | Why | ✅ Instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Best in the city" | Unprovable, everyone says it | "[N]+ customers in [City]" |
| "Limited time offer!!!" | Reads as a scam | "Offer valid till [DATE]" |
| "Guaranteed results" | Policy risk | "[Specific past result]" |
| "Affordable rates" | Meaningless | "Starting ₹[X]" |
| "Contact us for details" | Extra friction step | State the details |
| "DM for price" | Frustrating, loses people | Show the price |
| ALL CAPS SENTENCES | Reads as spam | Normal case |
| Excessive emoji | Unprofessional | 1–3, functional only |
