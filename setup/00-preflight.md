# 00 — Preflight Checklist

**Do not create a campaign until every box that applies to you is ticked.** Every item here is something that, if missing, wastes real money.

---

## Tier 1 — Absolutely required

- [ ] **Facebook Page**, published, with profile photo and cover image
  → [`01-page-and-business-manager.md`](01-page-and-business-manager.md)
- [ ] **Meta Business Manager** (business.facebook.com) with your Page and ad account inside it
- [ ] **Ad account** created, currency set to **INR**, timezone **Asia/Kolkata**
  > ⚠️ Currency and timezone are **permanent**. You cannot change them later — you'd have to create a new ad account. Check twice.
- [ ] **Working payment method**, verified with a real charge
  → [`02-payments-india-gst.md`](02-payments-india-gst.md)
- [ ] **You know what one customer is worth to you in ₹** (revenue × margin)
  > Without this you cannot compute a budget, and any number you pick is arbitrary

## Tier 2 — Required for your chosen playbook

## Tier 3 — Strongly recommended

- [ ] **Instagram professional account** linked to the Page — doubles your placements at no extra cost
- [ ] **Two-factor authentication** on your personal Facebook login
  > If your personal account is compromised or disabled, you lose access to the ad account. This happens more than people expect
- [ ] **A second admin** on the Business Manager — a trusted person, not an agency you just met
- [ ] **Payment backup method** — failed payments pause campaigns mid-flight and reset momentum
- [ ] **A capacity plan for replies.** Who answers enquiries, and how fast?

---

## The reply-capacity check

This is the most-skipped item and the most expensive.

```
Enquiries you expect per day:              ______
Minutes needed to handle one properly:     ______
Total minutes/day required:                ______
Minutes you actually have:                 ______
```

If required exceeds available, **do not launch yet.** Either reduce budget, or use a qualifying question to cut volume, or arrange help. Indian buyers typically contact 3–4 businesses simultaneously; the one who replies first usually wins. Unanswered leads are 100% wasted spend.

## Common preflight failures

| Failure | Consequence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Ad account currency set to USD | Every report in dollars; confusing budgets; you cannot change it | Create a new ad account with INR |
| Timezone not Asia/Kolkata | Daily budgets reset at the wrong hour; reports misaligned | Cannot change — new ad account |
| Personal profile used instead of Business Manager | Can't add team, can't separate assets, fragile | Create Business Manager, move assets |
| No privacy policy URL | Instant Form ads get rejected | Add a page to your site, or use a free generator |
| WhatsApp number already on a personal WhatsApp | Can't connect to Business app cleanly | Use a separate number for business |
| Page has no posts | Ads look untrustworthy; people check the Page | Post 5–10 genuine pieces first |
| No 2FA | Account takeover risk | Enable now |

## Final gate — five questions

1. Can you state, in ₹, what one customer is worth? → if no, read [`../data/best-budget-recommender.md`](../data/best-budget-recommender.md)
2. Has your payment method been successfully charged at least once?
3. Does your Page look like a real business to a stranger?
4. Do you know exactly what action you want from the ad?
5. Can you respond to enquiries within 5 minutes during business hours?

**Five yeses → go to [`../playbooks/README.md`](../playbooks/README.md).**
