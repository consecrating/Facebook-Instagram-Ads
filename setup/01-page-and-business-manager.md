# 01 — Page, Instagram & Business Manager

---

## Why Business Manager, not just a Page

Running ads from a personal profile works until it doesn't. Business Manager (business.facebook.com) gives you: separation of business and personal assets, the ability to add people without sharing your login, multiple ad accounts, and a recovery path if one person's access breaks.

**Set it up before you spend money, not after.** Migrating assets later is painful.

---

## Step 1 — Facebook Page

**facebook.com/pages/create**

| Field | What to do |
|---|---|
| Page name | Your real business name. Avoid keyword stuffing — it looks like spam |
| Category | Closest genuine match |
| Bio | One clear line: what you do, for whom, where |
| Profile photo | Logo, 170×170 minimum, readable when small |
| Cover photo | 820×312. Your offer or your work, not a stock handshake |
| Contact | Phone, WhatsApp, email, website |
| Address | Required if you want footfall campaigns ([P06](../playbooks/P06-local-footfall.md)) |
| Hours | Set them accurately |

### Post 5–10 things before advertising

People click your Page name before they enquire. An empty Page kills trust and costs you conversions you already paid for.

Post: photos of actual work, a customer testimonial, your price list or range, a short intro video, an FAQ. Genuine and slightly rough beats polished and empty.

---

## Step 2 — Instagram professional account

Instagram placements roughly double your available inventory at no extra cost. Skipping this makes your ads more expensive for no reason.

1. Instagram app → **Settings → Account type → Switch to professional account**
2. Choose **Business** (not Creator) for most businesses
3. Complete the bio, add contact buttons, add a link
4. Connect to your Facebook Page: Settings → **Sharing and linking** / **Linked accounts**

> Instagram moves these menus regularly. Search settings for "professional" or "linked" if the path differs.

---

## Step 3 — Business Manager

1. **business.facebook.com** → **Create account**
2. Business name, your name, business email
3. Fill in the business details — address, website, entity type. Incomplete details can cause verification friction later

### Add your assets

**Business settings → Accounts:**
- **Pages** → Add → Add a Page you own (don't "request access" to your own Page)
- **Instagram accounts** → Add → log in
- **Ad accounts** → **Create a new ad account**

### Creating the ad account — the irreversible part

```
Ad account name:  [Business] - Ads
Time zone:        (GMT+05:30) Asia/Kolkata     ← PERMANENT
Currency:         INR - Indian Rupee            ← PERMANENT
```

> ⚠️ **Timezone and currency cannot be changed after creation.** Getting these wrong means creating a new ad account and losing accumulated history — which genuinely affects delivery cost. Read them twice before clicking Create.

---

## Step 4 — People and security

**Business settings → People → Add**

Roles: **Employee** for most, **Admin** only for people you'd trust with the business. Assign per-asset access rather than blanket admin.

Add at least one second admin — a co-founder or family member. If your account is ever disabled, a second admin is the difference between a delay and losing everything.

### Security, not optional

- **2FA on your personal Facebook login.** Your ad account is only as secure as the profile that controls it
- Never share passwords; use Business Manager roles
- If an agency asks for your login instead of Business Manager access, that's a bad sign
- Check **Business settings → Security Center** periodically

---

## Step 5 — Business verification

Meta may ask you to verify your business (documents proving the entity exists — GST certificate, incorporation documents, utility bill). It's required for some features and higher spend limits.

Do it early if you're a registered entity. It's slow when you're in a hurry, and it unblocks things you'll eventually want.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Page published, complete, with 5–10 genuine posts
- [ ] Instagram professional account, linked to the Page
- [ ] Business Manager created with real business details
- [ ] Page + Instagram + ad account all inside Business Manager
- [ ] **Ad account: INR + Asia/Kolkata, verified before creating**
- [ ] Second admin added
- [ ] 2FA enabled
- [ ] Business verification started, if applicable
