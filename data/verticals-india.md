# Indian Verticals — Questions, Not Assumptions

This file contains **no assumption about your business**. It's a lookup of what to ask and what to watch out for, per vertical. The AI assistant uses it to ask better questions; you use it to spot the trap specific to your industry.

Each entry gives: the right objective, the questions that must be answered, the vertical-specific trap, and the playbook.

---

## How to use this (for the AI assistant)

1. Find the user's vertical below (or the closest match)
2. Ask its **Must ask** questions — these are the inputs the budget engine needs
3. Warn them about the **Trap**
4. Route to the **Playbook**
5. If no vertical matches, use the generic block at the end. Do **not** force a bad match

## Local services
*plumbers, electricians, AC repair, pest control, packers & movers, carpenters*

- **Objective:** Engagement → WhatsApp
- **Must ask:** average job value ₹? · service radius km? · can you answer calls/chats during work hours?
- **Trap:** leads arrive while you're on a job and go unanswered. Cheap leads are worthless if nobody replies. Fix reply capacity *before* spending
- **Edge:** hyper-local radius targeting works genuinely well here — 5–15 km
- **Playbook:** [P00](../playbooks/P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md)

## Coaching, tuition, test prep, edtech
- **Objective:** Engagement → WhatsApp, or Leads → Instant Form for demo bookings
- **Must ask:** fee per student ₹? · batch size? · admission season timing? · online or centre-based? · parent or student is the decision-maker?
- **Trap:** targeting students when **parents** hold the money and make the decision. Age targeting is often wrong by 20 years
- **Edge:** demand is sharply seasonal — concentrate budget in admission windows rather than spreading year-round
- **Playbook:** [P00](../playbooks/P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md) · [P09](../playbooks/P09-appointment-booking.md) for demo classes

## Real estate & property
- **Objective:** Leads → Instant Form (Higher Intent), or WhatsApp
- **Must ask:** ticket size ₹? · project location? · site-visit-to-booking rate? · RERA number available?
- **Trap:** **Housing is a Special Ad Category in some jurisdictions** — check the declaration requirement carefully, because declaring it removes most targeting options. Also: high ticket size means high CPL is fine, but your learning-phase floor gets expensive fast
- **Edge:** site visit as the conversion event, not "lead". A booked visit is worth 10 form fills
- **Playbook:** [P02](../playbooks/P02-instant-form-leads-200.md) · [P09](../playbooks/P09-appointment-booking.md) · read [policy](../reference/policy-and-special-categories.md) **first**

## Healthcare, clinics, dental, diagnostics
- **Objective:** Engagement → WhatsApp, or appointment booking
- **Must ask:** consultation fee ₹? · lifetime patient value ₹? · which treatments can you legally advertise?
- **Trap:** **Meta policy on health claims is strict.** No before/after imagery, no guaranteed outcomes, no targeting that implies a medical condition. Rejections are common and repeated violations risk the ad account
- **Edge:** lead with the consultation, not the treatment. Compliant and converts better
- **Playbook:** [P09](../playbooks/P09-appointment-booking.md) · [policy](../reference/policy-and-special-categories.md) **mandatory**

## Salon, spa, beauty, wellness
- **Objective:** Engagement → WhatsApp, or footfall
- **Must ask:** average bill ₹? · repeat visit frequency? · location radius?
- **Trap:** before/after images are heavily restricted; also discount-led ads attract one-time bargain hunters who never return. Optimize for repeat-worthy customers, not the cheapest booking
- **Edge:** repeat frequency means you can pay more per first visit than you'd think — compute lifetime value, not first-ticket value
- **Playbook:** [P06](../playbooks/P06-local-footfall.md) · [P00](../playbooks/P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md)

## Restaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens
- **Objective:** Awareness/Traffic for footfall; Engagement → WhatsApp for orders
- **Must ask:** average order value ₹? · delivery radius? · aggregator (Swiggy/Zomato) or direct?
- **Trap:** margins are thin, so a ₹50 lead can be unprofitable. Also, driving traffic to aggregators means paying to acquire a customer you don't own
- **Edge:** very tight radius (3–7 km) plus mealtime dayparting
- **Playbook:** [P06](../playbooks/P06-local-footfall.md)

## D2C / e-commerce
- **Objective:** Sales; Engagement → WhatsApp for lower budgets
- **Must ask:** AOV ₹? · gross margin %? · COD or prepaid? · RTO/return rate %? · Pixel + CAPI installed?
- **Trap:** **COD and RTO destroy the maths.** If 30% of COD orders are returned, your real CPA is far above what Ads Manager shows. Optimizing for "Purchase" when a third don't stick is optimizing for the wrong thing
- **Edge:** at low budget, CTWA outperforms website purchase campaigns — you get a conversation, can confirm the order, and cut RTO
- **Playbook:** [P05](../playbooks/P05-ecommerce-sales.md) · [P11](../playbooks/P11-catalog-retargeting.md) · [P00](../playbooks/P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md) if budget is tight

## Gyms, fitness, yoga
- **Objective:** Engagement → WhatsApp, or trial booking
- **Must ask:** membership price ₹? · average retention months? · trial-to-member rate?
- **Trap:** body transformation and before/after imagery is restricted. January demand spikes then collapses
- **Edge:** sell the free trial, not the membership. Cheaper event, warmer lead
- **Playbook:** [P09](../playbooks/P09-appointment-booking.md) · [P00](../playbooks/P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md)

## B2B services, agencies, software
- **Objective:** Leads → Instant Form (Higher Intent), or website leads
- **Must ask:** contract value ₹? · sales cycle length? · decision-maker job title?
- **Trap:** Meta's job-title targeting in India is far weaker than LinkedIn's. High ticket + long cycle means Meta attribution will look bad even when it's working
- **Edge:** lead with a genuinely useful resource, not a "book a demo"
- **Playbook:** [P02](../playbooks/P02-instant-form-leads-200.md) · [P03](../playbooks/P03-website-leads.md)

## Creators, influencers, personal brands
- **Objective:** Engagement, or profile visits
- **Must ask:** how do you monetise? · current follower count? · posting frequency?
- **Trap:** buying followers who never engage. Follower count without engagement is a vanity metric that costs real money
- **Edge:** promote your genuinely best-performing organic post rather than making a new ad
- **Playbook:** [P04](../playbooks/P04-instagram-followers.md) · [P08](../playbooks/P08-engagement-social-proof.md)

## Financial services, loans, insurance, credit
- **Objective:** Leads → Instant Form
- **Must ask:** commission per conversion ₹? · are you a regulated entity? · which products?
- **Trap:** ⚠️ **Credit is a Special Ad Category.** You must declare it, and declaring removes age, gender and most detailed targeting. Plan for materially higher costs and read the policy file before building anything
- **Playbook:** [P02](../playbooks/P02-instant-form-leads-200.md) · [policy](../reference/policy-and-special-categories.md) **mandatory first**

## Recruitment, jobs, staffing
- **Objective:** Leads → Instant Form
- **Must ask:** fee per placement ₹? · roles and locations?
- **Trap:** ⚠️ **Employment is a Special Ad Category.** Declaration required; targeting restricted
- **Playbook:** [P02](../playbooks/P02-instant-form-leads-200.md) · [policy](../reference/policy-and-special-categories.md) **mandatory first**

## Travel, tours, hospitality
- **Objective:** Engagement → WhatsApp
- **Must ask:** package value ₹? · margin %? · booking lead time? · domestic or outbound?
- **Trap:** long consideration window means last-click attribution understates performance. People enquire in January for a May trip
- **Edge:** conversation-led selling suits high-consideration purchases — WhatsApp beats forms here
- **Playbook:** [P00](../playbooks/P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md) · [P07](../playbooks/P07-video-retarget-funnel-200.md)

## Events, weddings, photography
- **Objective:** Engagement → WhatsApp
- **Must ask:** booking value ₹? · season? · service radius?
- **Trap:** sharply seasonal — spreading budget across a full year wastes most of it
- **Edge:** portfolio video as creative; concentrate spend into the booking season
- **Playbook:** [P00](../playbooks/P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md)

## If your vertical isn't listed

Answer these and the engine still works:

```
1. What exactly do you sell?
2. Revenue from one closed customer            ₹ ______
3. Gross margin                                 ______%
4. Out of 10 enquiries, how many buy?           ______
5. What should someone DO after seeing the ad?
6. Where do you serve?                          ____________
7. Do you have: Page ☐  Instagram ☐  Website ☐  WhatsApp Business ☐  Pixel ☐
8. Real daily budget                           ₹ ______
9. Can you reply to enquiries within 5 minutes during business hours?
```

Questions 2–4 give the affordable CPL. Question 5 gives the optimization event. Question 9 decides whether WhatsApp is viable at all.

## Cross-vertical truths for India

1. **WhatsApp beats forms for anything needing a conversation.** Reported 30–50% lower CPL, and warmer leads
2. **Reply speed is a bigger lever than targeting.** 5 minutes vs 5 hours changes close rates more than any audience tweak
3. **Put the price in the ad.** Fewer, better-qualified enquiries at the same spend
4. **Tier-2/3 cities are cheaper** — if you can serve them, your budget stretches further
5. **Regional-language creative faces less competition** than English
6. **Festive and wedding seasons raise CPMs.** Plan around them
7. **Seasonal businesses should concentrate budget**, never spread it evenly
8. **Compute lifetime value, not first-transaction value**, wherever there's repeat purchase
