# P04 — Instagram Follower Growth

For creators, personal brands and local retail where an owned audience has real value.

## Read this before spending

**Meta has no "get followers" objective.** Anyone selling you a follower campaign is running something else and relabelling it. What you can actually do is put your content in front of the right people cheaply and let good content convert them.

**Followers are only worth buying if you monetise them.** Ask honestly: what is one follower worth to you in ₹? If you can't answer, spend the money on [P00](P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md) and get enquiries instead.

```
Monthly revenue from your audience      ₹ ______
Total followers                           ______
Revenue per follower per month          ₹ ______
Followers needed to matter                ______
```

If revenue per follower is near zero, this playbook is a vanity purchase.

**Works at ₹200/day:** yes. Engagement events are cheap, so the learning-phase floor is low (~₹50–200/day).

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] **Instagram professional account**, linked to your Facebook Page
- [ ] **At least 9–12 genuinely good posts.** People land on your profile and decide in seconds. Advertising into an empty profile wastes the whole budget
- [ ] A clear bio: who you are, what people get, one CTA
- [ ] Payment verified

---

## Step 1 — Pick your best organic post

Do **not** make a new ad. Open **Instagram Insights** and find the post with the highest **saves** and **shares** in the last 90 days.

Saves and shares predict follow-through far better than likes. A post people save is a post people want more of — which is exactly the follow decision.

Promoting proven organic content consistently beats promoting purpose-made ad creative here.

---

## Step 2 — Campaign

1. **Objective:** **Engagement**
2. **Name:** `IG | Follower Growth | Sep26`
3. **Advantage campaign budget:** OFF
4. **Next**

## Step 3 — Ad set

1. **Name:** `Broad | IG Profile | [Interest area]`
2. **Engagement type / Conversion location:** **Instagram profile visits** if available in your account; otherwise **On your post** (post engagement)
   > Profile visits is the closest available proxy for the follow action. If your account doesn't offer it, post engagement works — it just optimizes one step earlier.
3. **Performance goal:** maximise the selected event
4. **Budget:** Daily, ₹200
5. **Bid:** Highest volume
6. **Location:** where your audience actually is. For creators this may be all India; for local retail, your city
7. **Age / Gender:** match your genuine audience — check Instagram Insights for your existing follower demographics rather than guessing
8. **Advantage+ Audience:** **ON**, fields empty
   > This is one case where a *few* interest signals can help, because content relevance matters more than purchase intent. Add 2–3 at most if your niche is specific. Never stack ten.
9. **Placements:** **Manual → Instagram only**
   > The rare exception to the Advantage+ placements rule. You want Instagram Feed, Reels, Explore and Stories — not Facebook, where a click won't grow your Instagram
10. **Next**

## Step 4 — Ads

**Use existing post** — select the winner from Step 1.

- **Format:** Reels (9:16) generally reach furthest and cost least
- **CTA:** no hard CTA needed; the profile visit *is* the action
- **First line of caption:** make the value of following explicit
  ```
  Daily [TOPIC] tips for [AUDIENCE] in [CITY]. Follow for more →
  ```

Run **2–3 different proven posts** rather than three variations of one.

---

## Step 5 — Make the profile convert

Half of this playbook is off-platform. Traffic arrives; your profile decides.

- **Bio:** what they get, for whom, plus one CTA. Not a mission statement
- **Profile photo:** recognisable at thumbnail size
- **Pinned posts:** your three best — this is your shop window
- **Highlights:** organised, covered, useful
- **Grid consistency:** a visitor should understand your niche in three seconds
- **Post regularly** during the campaign. Followers gained from an inactive account unfollow quickly

## Measurement contract

Instagram doesn't attribute followers to ads directly, so measure manually.

### Baseline, before launching
```
Date:                    ______
Follower count:          ______
Average daily growth:    ______
```

### Day 7
```
Follower count now:              ______
Net new followers:               ______
Minus expected organic growth:   ______   ← be honest
Ad-attributable followers:       ______
Spend:                          ₹______
Cost per follower:              ₹______
```

### Judge it
| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| Cost per follower well below your revenue per follower | Working — continue |
| Followers growing but engagement rate falling | Wrong audience — tighten interests |
| Profile visits high, follows low | **Profile problem**, not an ads problem — fix bio and pinned posts |
| Barely any movement | Content isn't compelling enough to pay for. Improve organic first |

**Also track engagement rate**, not just count. 1,000 engaged followers beat 10,000 dead ones — and Instagram's distribution rewards the former.

---

## What not to do

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| Buy followers from third-party sellers | Fake accounts, kills your engagement rate, risks the account |
| Run follower ads with fewer than 9 posts | Nothing to convince anyone |
| Use Facebook placements for Instagram growth | Wrong platform for the action |
| Chase follower count with no monetisation | You're buying a number |
| Target broad "all India" for a local shop | Followers who can never visit you |
