# P08 — Engagement & Social Proof

Make your Page and ads look credible **before** you spend money asking for leads.

**Works at ₹200/day:** yes — engagement events are cheap, so the learning floor is low (~₹50–200/day).

---

## Why this is worth doing first

A stranger considering your ad does two things: glances at your engagement, and sometimes clicks your Page. An ad with 0 likes and no comments reads as untrustworthy — and in India, where scepticism about online sellers is high, that scepticism is expensive.

**This playbook is cheap insurance for every campaign that follows.** Run it for 7–14 days before or alongside a lead campaign.

**But be clear on what it is:** engagement is not a business outcome. Don't run this for months and call it marketing. It's a credibility layer, and it should be time-boxed.

---

## The two things it produces

1. **Visible social proof** — likes, comments, shares on ads you'll reuse later
2. **A retargeting audience** — everyone who engaged becomes a warm audience for [P00](P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md) or [P07](P07-video-retarget-funnel-200.md)

The second is the more valuable and the more overlooked.

4. **Next**

## Step 2 — Ad set

1. **Name:** `Broad | Post Engagement | [Area]`
2. **Engagement type:** **On your post** (post engagement)
3. **Performance goal:** **Maximise engagement**
4. **Budget:** Daily, **₹100–200**
5. **Bid:** Highest volume
6. **Location:** your service area
7. **Age / Gender:** wide / All
8. **Advantage+ Audience:** ON, fields empty
9. **Placements:** Advantage+ ON
10. **Next**

## Step 3 — Ads

### Critical: use the same post across campaigns to accumulate proof

When you later run a lead campaign, use **Use existing post** and select the *same* post. All the engagement carries over — your lead ad launches already looking established.

**How:** create the post on your Page → note its Post ID → in future ads choose **Use existing post → Enter post ID**.

This is the single most useful mechanic in this playbook. Engagement compounds onto one asset instead of scattering across throwaway creatives.

### What to promote

Pick content people actually engage with, not ads:

| Content type | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Genuinely useful tip | Saves and shares |
| Before/after of your *work* (not bodies) | Comments |
| Customer testimonial | Trust |
| "Ask me anything" about your field | Comments |
| Behind the scenes | Relatability |
| Price transparency post | Shares — rare and appreciated |

**Avoid:** pure promotion, discount-only posts, anything that reads as an advertisement. Those get low engagement per rupee, which defeats the purpose.

## Step 4 — Build the audiences (do this on Day 1)

**Audiences → Create audience → Custom audience**

| Audience | Source | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| `Page engagers - 365d` | Facebook Page | 365 days |
| `IG engagers - 365d` | Instagram account | 365 days |
| `Video 50% - 365d` | Video (if you ran video) | 365 days |

These are the assets. In 30 days you'll have a warm pool that makes every later campaign cheaper.

---

## Step 5 — Manage the comments

Ads with comments get engagement; ads with *unanswered* comments get damaged.

- **Reply to every comment.** Especially "price?" — reply publicly with the range, then "DM/WhatsApp for details"
- **Never delete a genuine negative comment.** Reply professionally; onlookers judge your response more than the complaint
- **Hide spam and abuse** (hiding keeps it visible to the commenter, avoiding a fight)
- **Comment-to-lead is real:** many enquiries come from comment threads. Watch them

**Set up a moderation filter:** Page settings → block obvious spam keywords. On a low budget you can't afford to police threads all day.

## Measurement contract

### Day 7
```
Spend:                       ₹______
Post engagements:             ______
Cost per engagement:         ₹______
Page engagers audience size:  ______
Comments received:            ______
Enquiries from comments/DMs:  ______
```

**The number that matters is the audience size**, not the engagement count. You're building a retargeting pool.

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| Audience past ~1,000 | **Stop or reduce this.** Move budget to [P00](P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md) or [P07](P07-video-retarget-funnel-200.md) retargeting that pool |
| Cheap engagement, no audience growth | Wrong engagement type — switch to video views |
| Expensive engagement | Content isn't interesting enough. Change the post, not the targeting |
| Getting enquiries directly | Bonus — but move to a lead objective to get more |

### Time-box it
**Don't run this beyond 2–4 weeks.** Once you have social proof and an audience, it has done its job. Continuing to buy engagement indefinitely is buying a metric.

## If you specifically want *likes*

This playbook is where "getting likes" belongs — but be clear about what likes are for.

**Likes are worth buying for exactly two reasons:**
1. **Social proof** on a post you'll reuse in later ad campaigns (via the Post ID mechanic above)
2. **A retargetable audience** of people who engaged

**Likes are not a business outcome.** Meta's delivery does exactly what you ask: optimize for likes and it finds people who are good at clicking like — a different population from people who buy.

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| Likes/comments/shares on posts | **This playbook** — both platforms, Advantage+ placements ON |
| Facebook **Page** likes/followers | [`P13`](P13-facebook-page-likes-followers.md) — Facebook-only placements |
| Instagram followers | [`P04`](P04-instagram-followers.md) — Instagram-only placements |
| Actual enquiries and customers | [`P00`](P00-BEST-low-budget-campaign.md) |
